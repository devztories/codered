CODERED INVENTIONS - UPDATE NOTES

This archive preserves the original uploaded project and its Git history.

Applied updates:
- HTML responses receive no-store cache headers to reduce stale pages on browser Back/refresh.
- Profile update uses POST-Redirect-GET.
- Payment proof submission redirects to Order Detail.
- Existing submitted/verified payment redirects away from the QR payment page.
- Order detail uses payment_submitted to show the payment queue state.

Important: Review other customer form views individually for POST-Redirect-GET. Global cache headers alone do not prevent duplicate submissions.
